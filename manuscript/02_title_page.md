The analysis used de-identified secondary microdata released to the authors on
application by the Korea Institute for Health and Social Affairs under a data-use
agreement. Informed consent for the survey was obtained by the data producers from all
participating households at the time of data collection. The authors did not have
access to any information that could identify individual participants.
